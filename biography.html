Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology. My research lies at the intersection of optimization and statistical learning, with a focus on developing scalable mixed-integer optimization methods for statistical learning problems. Prior to joining Georgia Tech, I completed my B.Tech. and M.Tech. (by Advanced Standing) in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi), where I was awarded the Institute Silver Medal for graduating with the highest cumulative GPA in my undergraduate cohort.
